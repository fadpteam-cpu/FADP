Riverdale Art Center

An art centre that is public from the pavement — a room you can see into, studios above, a courtyard as the interior street.

An art centre has to work from the pavement. The corner is glazed as a room, not a lobby. Studios sit above so the street can see work being made. That is the public contract.

The stair holds the courtyard. Plans put public room, shop, studio, gallery and terrace in sequence — you move through rooms, not a corridor of doors. A cultural building study. No area or cost is claimed.

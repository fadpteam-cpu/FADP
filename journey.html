Home · Your project, step by step

What happens, from first call to finished project.

Every project runs the same clear path. Explore each stage below to see exactly what we do, what you do, and where you can stop. No jargon, no surprises.

01Week 1 · No charge

Free consultation

A conversation about your project, your property and your budget. No cost, no obligation.

What we do

  • Meet you at the studio, on site, or by video call
  • Listen to what you want to achieve and what you're worried about
  • Give you an honest first read on whether it's likely to get planning
  • Explain the route, the rough cost, and the realistic timeline

What you do

  • Tell us what you're hoping to do, and your budget if you have one
  • Share any documents you already have (deeds, past drawings, surveys)
  • Ask everything — no question is too basic
✕
Can you stop here? Nothing is committed. You leave with a written note of where you stand and a fixed fee for the next stage, and you decide in your own time.
02Weeks 2–6 · Fixed fee, agreed first

Feasibility & design

We test what can actually be built, then design two or three options to your brief. You choose the direction before anything goes further.

What we do

  • Measure and survey the existing building or site
  • Test what's possible against the local planning policy
  • Design two to three real options, with drawings you can understand
  • Band the likely build cost for each, so you can choose with your eyes open

What you do

  • React to the options honestly — tell us what you love and what you don't
  • Confirm your budget and priorities so we design to them
  • Pick the direction you want to take forward
✕
Can you stop here? You can stop at the end of this stage with a set of drawings and a clear understanding of what your property can do — useful even if you pause the project for a year.
03Weeks 6–16 · Fixed fee, agreed first

Planning & approvals

We prepare and submit the application, handle the council, and manage every question through to a decision.

What we do

  • Prepare the full application and any supporting statements
  • Submit to the council and manage the case officer throughout
  • Respond to questions, objections and conditions on your behalf
  • Keep you updated in plain language at every step — no silence

What you do

  • Approve the final drawings before we submit
  • Sit back — this is the stage we take off your plate entirely
  • Talk to neighbours early if we advise it (we'll guide you)
✕
Can you stop here? Most householder applications are decided within eight weeks of validation. If approval matters before you commit further, you can stop here with a consent that adds value to your property.
04After approval · Fixed fee, agreed first

Technical design

The drawings that turn a planning permission into something a builder can actually price and build — structure, regulations, details.

What we do

  • Produce building regulations drawings and specifications
  • Coordinate the structural engineer and any other consultants
  • Prepare a tender package so builders price the same thing
  • Take formal Principal Designer duties where they apply

What you do

  • Make your final choices on materials, layouts and finishes
  • Review the package before it goes out to builders
✕
Can you stop here? You can stop here with a complete, priced-ready package and take it to builders yourself, or ask us to carry on and run the build.
05To completion · Fixed fee, agreed first

On site to completion

The build itself, with a director inspecting on site, through to the certificates you keep for resale.

What we do

  • Help you tender and appoint the right builder
  • Inspect the work on site as it progresses
  • Deal with the questions and decisions that come up during the build
  • See you through to completion and sign-off

What you do

  • Choose your builder with our guidance
  • Enjoy watching it take shape — we handle the technical side
✕
Can you stop here? This is completion: your finished project, with the certificates and records you'll need if you ever sell.

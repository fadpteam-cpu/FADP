BIM coordination

The studio works in BIM as standard, with federated models, clash detection and digital handover to Level 2.

The studio designs in BIM as standard, working within a single coordinated model rather than a set of separate drawings.

The brief+

The studio designs in BIM as standard, working within a single coordinated model rather than a set of separate drawings.

Approach+

Every element carries information about what it is and how it connects, so that drawings are consistent, quantities are reliable and coordination between disciplines happens in design rather than on site.

Planning and regulation+

The method follows the UK BIM framework and is applied to every project, including those where it is not mandated.

Delivery+

Models are federated with consultants' information, checked for clashes and handed over in digital form at completion.

Fees+

Fees are fixed for each stage and confirmed in writing once the scope is agreed. Construction cost depends on the site, the specification and the procurement route, and is established through an indicative cost plan at feasibility stage.

Common questions

Is the project presented in 3D? +

Yes, at every design stage. Most clients find decisions much easier in 3D than on plan.

Does BIM cost more? +

No. Modelling sits inside our fixed design fee. On balance it saves money by tighter tender pricing and fewer variations on site.

Do contractors need BIM software? +

No. Contractors receive conventional drawings and schedules. The BIM benefit is baked in.

What is BIM Level 2? +

The UK standard for centrally-procured public projects since 2016. The studio applies the method to every project, including those where it is not mandated.

Can BIM handle existing and heritage buildings? +

Yes. The case is often stronger — a model of the existing fabric makes coordination with new work possible.

Who owns the model? +

Typically the client, on payment in full, with the practice retaining design copyright. Same principle as conventional drawings.

What happens to it after completion? +

Handed over as part of completion documentation. Sensible clients keep it for future alterations and asset management.

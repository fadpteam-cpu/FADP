Dropped kerb applications

Applications and drawings for driveway access — coordinated with highways, first-time-right.

What a dropped kerb application involves+

A dropped kerb converts raised pavement kerb to a vehicle crossing. It requires a highways application, not a planning application. We check sight lines, junction distances, parking depth, drainage, and street trees before submitting — the common reasons for refusal. One application, submitted correctly, approved first time.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a dropped kerb cost? +
Our design and application fee: £750-£1,500. Council construction costs: £1,500-£3,000. Total typically £2,500-£4,500.
How long does a dropped kerb take? +
Typically 4-8 weeks from application to decision. Council may inspect the site before deciding.

Fixed fees in writing. First consultation free.

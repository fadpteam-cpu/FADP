Party wall awards

Notices drafted and served under the Party Wall etc. Act 1996 — coordinated with your appointed surveyor, handled properly from the start.

When you need a party wall notice+

You must serve a party wall notice on your neighbour if your proposed works involve:

Work on or to a party wall — cutting into, raising, underpinning, or demolishing a wall shared with a neighbour (Section 2 notice, 2 months before work starts).

Excavation within 3 or 6 metres — digging foundations, basements, or drainage within 3m of a neighbouring building, or within 6m if the excavation goes below a 45-degree line drawn from the base of the neighbour's foundations (Section 6 notice, 1 month before work starts).

Building a new wall on the boundary — a new wall built on or astride the boundary line (Section 1 notice, 1 month before work starts).

On a London terrace, nearly every extension triggers at least one party wall notice — usually Section 6 (excavation near the neighbour's foundations) and often Section 2 (work on the party wall itself).

What we do+

We draft the notices, ensure they include the correct drawings and descriptions, and coordinate the serving process. If your neighbour consents in writing, the process is simple and cheap. If they dissent (or fail to respond within 14 days, which counts as dissent), both sides appoint surveyors to agree a Party Wall Award — a binding document that sets out the works, protective measures, and access arrangements.

The key is timing. Party wall notices must be served before work starts — not during construction. Late or missing notices can result in injunctions, stop-work orders, and liability for any damage caused to the neighbour's property.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a party wall notice cost? +
Our fee for drafting and coordinating party wall notices is £450-£750 per neighbour. If the neighbour dissents and surveyors are appointed, surveyor fees are additional — typically £1,500-£3,000 per award. The surveyor fees are payable by you (the building owner), not the neighbour.
How long does the party wall process take? +
Minimum 2 months for a Section 2 notice, 1 month for Sections 1 and 6. If the neighbour dissents and surveyors are appointed, the award typically takes a further 4-8 weeks. Start early — it's the most common cause of project delays.
What happens if my neighbour refuses to agree? +
If they don't respond within 14 days, it's treated as dissent. Both sides appoint surveyors (or agree a single "agreed surveyor") who produce a Party Wall Award. The process continues regardless of the neighbour's cooperation — it's designed to prevent one party from blocking the other.

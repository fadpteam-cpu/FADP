Party wall awards

Notices prepared and served under the Party Wall etc. Act 1996, coordinated with the appointed surveyor from the outset.

The brief+

The studio prepares and serves notices under the Party Wall etc. Act 1996, coordinated with the design and with the appointed surveyors.

Approach+

Early and considered notification reduces the risk of dispute and delay. Notices are prepared from the design drawings so that the works described are accurate and complete.

Planning and regulation+

Where neighbours consent, the process is concluded promptly; where they dissent, surveyors are appointed to prepare an award.

Delivery+

The studio coordinates the process with the programme for the works.

Fees+

Fees are fixed for each stage and confirmed in writing once the scope is agreed. Construction cost depends on the site, the specification and the procurement route, and is established through an indicative cost plan at feasibility stage.

Frequently asked questions

How are fees and construction costs established? +
Fees are fixed for each stage and confirmed in writing once the scope is agreed. Construction cost depends on the site, the specification and the procurement route, and is established through an indicative cost plan at feasibility stage.
How long does the party wall process take? +
Minimum 2 months for a Section 2 notice, 1 month for Sections 1 and 6. If the neighbour dissents and surveyors are appointed, the award typically takes a further 4-8 weeks. Start early — it is the most common cause of project delays.
What happens if a neighbour does not agree? +
If they do not respond within 14 days, it is treated as dissent. Both sides appoint surveyors (or agree a single "agreed surveyor") who produce a Party Wall Award. The process continues regardless of the neighbour's cooperation — it is designed to prevent one party from blocking the other.

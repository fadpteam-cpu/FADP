# FADP positioning

## The idea
FADP is a planning-led design studio for difficult sites: protected, constrained or in operation.
The planning, the design and the delivery are resolved before anything is built.

Every page, service, project and piece of writing should support this idea. If it does not, it does not go on the site.

## Three disciplines, one appointment
- **Planning.** The planning position is established before design begins, and treated as part of the design.
- **Design.** Every design begins with the site: its context, its constraints and the way it is used.
- **Delivery.** Technical design, principal designer duties and contract administration carry the design through to completion.

## Six areas of work
1. Planning and consents
2. Homes
3. Heritage
4. Masterplanning
5. Visitor attractions
6. Technical and principal designer

Smaller services (vehicle access, party wall notices, chimney and wall removal, crack inspections, snagging and similar) sit inside these areas. They keep their pages for search but are not presented as the identity of the studio.

## Two audiences
- **Private clients** with homes on constrained or protected sites.
- **Operators and developers** with larger or sensitive sites, including visitor attractions.

## Evidence
- Realised work is shown first. Concept and study projects are always labelled as such.
- Attraction and heritage projects are published only with the client's written permission.

## What we call ourselves
- "Design studio" or "studio". Do not use "architect" or "architects" unless the directors are registered with the Architects Registration Board. If registration is obtained, add the registration number to the footer and use the title.
- "FADP Architecture" is the trading name of Fa Design Partners Limited.

## Voice
- Third person ("the studio") or measured first person plural. Short, precise sentences. Principles rather than promises.
- No prices, no offers of free work, no claims about added property value, no superlatives.
- Sized honestly: a two-director studio founded in 2026. Specific rather than grand.

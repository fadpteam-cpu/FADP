About

A small studio, built on clarity.

The difference between a good project and a difficult one is rarely the design.

It's certainty.

FADP is a small London architecture practice founded in 2026 by Aun Naeem and Fatima Shakeel. We work on residential, commercial and heritage projects across the United Kingdom — every one led personally by a director.

How we work

Problem-solving first.

We start with the problem, not the drawing. Every brief begins with what you're trying to achieve.

Technical accuracy.

Details resolved before they reach site. Fewer surprises, fewer disputes, less cost.

Fast response.

One working day. Every enquiry gets a reply from a director, personally.

Direct access.

The same person you meet at first briefing runs the project through to completion.

Regular dialogue.

You always know where things stand. Progress updates in plain English, not jargon.

Fixed fees.

Written before we begin. If scope changes, we agree the adjustment before doing the work.

Directors

Aun Naeem

Director · Design & masterplanning
aun@fadp.co.uk

Aun leads FADP's design and masterplanning work. Educated at Birmingham School of Architecture and the University of Sheffield (MArch), his work spans cultural, educational and retrofit projects alongside international research. He brings a design-first approach grounded in careful analysis of place, context and use.

Fatima Shakeel

Director · Planning & delivery
fatima@fadp.co.uk

Fatima leads FADP's planning strategy and technical delivery. Educated at Birmingham School of Architecture, her experience covers cultural, religious, commercial and residential schemes across the UK and abroad. She is known for direct communication and technical rigour from planning stage through to construction.

INTRODUCER PROGRAMME

Introduce a client. Earn a commission.

Surveyors, contractors, trades and anyone in the industry. Send a homeowner who appoints FADP, and we pay you a written commission on our professional fee for that job.

Who it is for

Surveyors, contractors, builders, trades, estate agents — anyone who sits next to a homeowner who needs drawings or a planning pack. London first. Manchester and Birmingham the same way.

The rate, plainly

The rate is a commission on FADP’s professional fee — the number in our appointment letter, not a percentage of build cost. It is written in a one-page introducer letter before we take the meeting. Paid when the client pays us for that stage.

In writing
a commission on our professional fee
Not a percentage of build cost. The figure is in the letter before the first meeting.

How it works

01

Email the name, the address, and that you are introducing them.

02

We send the introducer letter. You sign it. Then we take the meeting.

03

If they appoint us, you earn the commission written in the letter.

04

We pay you when the client pays us for that stage.

The rules

  • The client can know. We will say an introducer is involved.
  • First signed letter wins if two people send the same lead.
  • No appointment, no commission.
  • No commission on work they already had with us.
  • You cannot introduce your own job and collect the fee.
  • No signed letter before the meeting means the lead is not counted.

Expertise

Concept & feasibility

Every project begins with a close reading of the site: its context, constraints and potential. Options are tested against planning policy and indicative cost, so that design proceeds from a sound brief.

Pre & planning applications

Planning is treated as part of the design, not a stage that follows it. The studio prepares and manages householder, full, outline and prior approval applications across London and the wider UK, through to the discharge of conditions.

Building regulations & construction

Technical design, structural coordination and contract administration carry the design through to site, so that what is approved is what is built.

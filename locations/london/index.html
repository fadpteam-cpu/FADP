Residential architecture in London

Studio at 66 Paul Street, EC2A 4NA. Extensions, lofts, planning and listed buildings — director-led, fixed fees.

London homeowners do not need another generic “designers in London” page. They need a practice that already knows why Camden refuses a box dormer, why Islington cares about the first-floor cill, and why a Hackney mansard lives or dies on the SPD and the street.

That is our core market. The studio sits on Paul Street, between Shoreditch and the City. Site visits across inner London are normal, not extras. We take planning applications, feasibility, listed building consent and principal designer appointments from first conversation through technical design.

Boroughs we publish

Camden

Conservation areas, Article 4, Hampstead to Kentish Town.

Islington

Georgian and Victorian terraces, tight rear gardens.

Hackney

Lofts, mansards, side-returns, refusal recoveries.

How we work in London

A director on the first call. Constraints on the table in week one. Three options where they help you decide. A written fixed fee before Stage 1. Planning drawings that are technically accurate enough to protect the budget, not just pretty enough to lodge.

Common questions

Where is the studio? 66 Paul Street, London EC2A 4NA.

Which boroughs do you focus on? Camden, Islington and Hackney first, then neighbouring inner-London boroughs where the project is a good fit.

Do you only do London work? No. Manchester and Birmingham are service areas with site visits.
